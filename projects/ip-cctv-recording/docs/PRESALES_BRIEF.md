# Technical demonstration and handover brief

## Demonstration objective

Show a prospective reviewer that live video, recorded playback, export, viewer permissions and source-fault recovery are separate acceptance concerns. Use two generated streams on a laptop; explain the result as an educational software demonstration.

## Questions to establish before a real proposal

What must the customer see and record? Which areas, lighting conditions and subjects matter? How many channels, what retention period, which authorised viewers and what failure tolerance are required? What equipment, network capacity, power, cable routes, recorder compatibility and maintenance arrangements exist? The current lab supplies none of those site measurements or supplier approvals.

## Demonstration sequence

1. Explain the two-source/one-recorder diagram.
2. Show the recorded acceptance table and distinguish successful video access from expected access denial.
3. Play each saved export and identify the generated moving patterns.
4. Show the camera outage and recorder restart results. Explain buffering and the evidence limits.
5. State the exact software scope and the physical tests needed before a real installation could be accepted.

## Handover package

Source and tool references, topology, before/after and recovery reports, selected redacted decoder/request logs, two actual exported videos and file hashes are included. Passwords, private configuration files, raw continuous recordings and academic records are excluded. A new run must generate fresh credentials and preserve its own results.

## Customer-facing explanation

“I ran a guided software CCTV demonstration using two generated video sources. I checked saved playback and exported videos as well as live streaming. The test isolated one source's outage and verified recorder restart recovery. I have not tested a physical camera or commercial NVR in this project.”

This is a suggested accurate explanation of validated work, not evidence of a customer meeting, sale, supplier quotation or commercial handover.
